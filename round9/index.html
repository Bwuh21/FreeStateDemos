Plan a visit

What your first Sunday feels like.

No visitor section, no standing up to introduce yourself. Come as you are. Shorts are fine.

  • Walking in

    Someone at the door will say hi and point you to the coffee. Sit anywhere.

  • Your kids

    Classes from 3 months through 5th grade. We’ll walk you to check-in. They’re optional.

  • The service

    A few songs, communion together, then a message straight from the Bible. Guests are welcome at communion and never expected to take it.

  • After

    Done by 11:30. Most people hang out on the lawn. Stay or head out, both are fine.

What we believe

The Bible, in ordinary words.

Love God. Love others. Change the world.

God is good, and mighty to save.
Not distant, not disappointed. Able to redeem the parts of your story that feel unfixable.
Knowing God changes people.
A real relationship with him brings fulfillment and changes us from the inside out.
Grace, through faith in Jesus, at baptism.
Salvation is received, never earned. We’d love to open the Bible and show you where that comes from.
Life to the full, starting now.
Following Jesus is about this life too, not only the next one.
