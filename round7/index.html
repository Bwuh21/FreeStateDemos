plan a visit what's sunday like?

a church full of regular people who love god and love each other.

Students from KU, families, lifers, and people who walked in last month. Some of us wear shorts to church. Nobody minds.

If you’re new, we built this page for you. Start with what an actual Sunday looks like.

your first sunday

here’s the whole morning, start to finish.

9:45 · doors & coffee

Park anywhere. Someone at the door will say hi and point you to the coffee.

Got kids? Classes run from 3 months through 5th grade. We’ll walk you to check-in, and they’re optional.

10:00 · worship

A few songs, led by people from the church. Sing along, listen, or just take it in. Lyrics are on the screen.

communion, every week

We share communion every Sunday. Guests are always welcome to take part and never expected to.

An offering basket goes by once. It’s for members. Let it pass. Nobody’s watching.

the message

A message straight from the Bible, aimed at real life on Monday. Bring a Bible if you have one. Pens and highlighters are popular.

Middle schoolers stay through communion, then head to their own class.

11:30 · the lawn

Service ends on time. Most people hang around outside with a coffee anyway. Stay as long as you like, or head out. Both are fine.
