Your first Sunday

Here’s exactly what happens.

No surprises. Nobody will make you stand up, introduce yourself, or fill out a card.

  1. 9:45

    Walk in

    Doors open at 9:45. Park anywhere. Someone will say hello and show you the coffee.

    Kids’ classes run from 3 months to 5th grade. They’re optional, and we’ll walk you to check-in.

  2. 10:00

    Worship and communion

    A few songs led by people from the church. Then communion, which we share every week. Guests are welcome and never expected to take part.

    An offering basket goes by once. It’s for members. Let it pass.

  3. By 11:30

    A message, then home

    A message straight from the Bible, for real life. We finish by 11:30, and plenty of people stay on the lawn to talk.

Love God. Love others. Change the world.

  • God is good, and mighty to save.

    Able to redeem the parts of your story that feel unfixable.

  • A relationship with God changes everything.

    It brings real fulfillment and transforms our lives.

  • Saved by grace.

    By the grace of God, through faith in Jesus, at baptism.

  • Life to the full.

    Jesus came so that we might have life to the full (John 10:10), starting now.
